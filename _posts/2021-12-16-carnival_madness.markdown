![Carnival Madness gameplay screenshot](/assets/images/game_carnival_madness_screen1.jpg)

Carnival Madness is a roguelike puzzle game set in a cursed carnival. You're trapped in a carnival that is slowly siphoning your soul, and the only way out is through a maze of portals. Each portal has to be unlocked with a combination of items, which you get by making trades. It's a roguelike, so it can be tough, but spotting patterns in the item combinations and event cards is the key to escaping.

It was an experimental project and my first published game, made in Unity with my friends Jordan Atwill and Francis De Luca. I led the team, oversaw the programming and design direction, and made all of the art.
