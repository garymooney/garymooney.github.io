![The FlowMenu website showing tonight's top picks](/assets/images/flowmenu-screenshot.jpg)

[FlowMenu](https://flowmenu.au) answers a simple question: *what's on in Melbourne this week that I'd actually enjoy?* It brings together events from across the city, works out what each one is really like, and ranks them against your preferences. It's a solo side project that I design, build and run myself.

### What it does

- **Casts a wide net.** It covers everything from small independent venues and council events to arts organisations and ticketed shows, so the smaller events don't get lost.
- **Describes each event.** Every listing gets a short, consistent summary and category, so it's easy to skim.
- **Ranks to your taste.** You can adjust your preferences in the settings and watch the list re-rank instantly.
- **Makes it easy to plan.** You can browse by day, by category, or on a map, with top picks highlighted for each night.

### Built with

Python, SQLite, local AI models, and a JavaScript/CSS front end.
