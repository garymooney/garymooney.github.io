  • 5 world records for large-scale entanglement detection on IBM Quantum hardware, up to 414 qubits
  • Industry collaboration with Ford on quantum optimisation for automotive manufacturing
  • 1st place at the Qiskit Hackathon Melbourne 2022 with qMuVi, now part of the IBM Qiskit Ecosystem
